# Team — advisor spec and hiring plan (addresses COM-015)

## The gate

Solo founders graduate seed → Series A at ~12.9% versus ~23.7% for two
co-founders (Carta dataset). The penalty is at the gate, not in the terms:
conditional on raising, solo valuations match non-solo. So the instruction is
narrow: get one more credible name with real ownership or a real title into
the room before the raise.

## Option ladder (first is best)

1. **Co-founder with security credibility.** Target profile: ex-security
   engineering leadership (CrowdStrike/SentinelOne/Fleet/CISA-adjacent),
   wants the agent-governance problem, available for 40/25/15/10/5/5 execution.
   Title: CTO or Co-founder & Head of Security Evidence.

2. **Named operating advisor with a title and time commitment.** Not a
   logo loan. Spec: 0.25–1.0% equity on a 2-year vest, 4+ hrs/wk, named in
   the data room and deck, owns SOC 2 readiness or GTM intro motion.

3. **Venture partner / strategic angel with a security thesis.** Person who
   can credibly answer "why not everybody else" in a partner meeting.

## Where to look (concrete)

- Security-mailing-list and conference judges already adjacent to the
  estate's public work (WarHacker contacts, DEF CON AI Village).
- The 20 design-partner discovery calls (BUYER_PERSONA.md) — the strongest
  co-founder leads in security companies come from buyers who say "I wish
  someone built this right."
- Open-source contributors to in-toto / sigstore / SLSA communities who
  already care about portable evidence.

## What the fill looks like in this ledger

When a name is real: update data_room/08_Team with a signed advisor or
employment agreement reference, then set COM-015 from UNKNOWN to their name.
The gate flips on the name, not on the intent.
