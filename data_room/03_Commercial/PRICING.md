# Pricing — v0 hypotheses (publish, then test)

Every price here is a **hypothesis to validate with design partners**, not a
claim of market truth. A wrong price you can defend beats no price. These
figures exist so that gross margin, CAC payback, and burn multiple become
computable for the first time.

## The wedge

Governed agent change management: production signal → agent investigation →
policy evaluation → human approval where required → bounded patch → tests →
deploy → observation window → signed closure/rollback receipt.

## SKUs

| Tier | Annual hypothesis | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **Verify** | Free / open | The `GovernedAction/v1` format, reference verifier, Article 12 conformance profile. Adoption play — give away the format. |
| **Control — design partner** | $50K–$150K | One production workflow wired end-to-end, receipts into your retention, weekly review. 6-month engagement, public testimonial rights. |
| **Control — department** | $75K–$250K | Multi-workflow control plane, evidence obligations, deny-path governance, offline auditor export. |
| **Assurance — enterprise** | $250K–$750K+ | Cross-org evidence graph, conformance tooling, Article 12 reporting, SSO/audit integrations, SOC 2 evidence pack. |
| **Sovereign** | $500K–$1M+ yr 1 | Air-gapped / UDS-Zarf deployment, own-metal mesh integration, dedicated conformance engineering. |

## What we never price on

- **Tokens.** The value is control, auditability, and deployment enablement —
  not inference volume. Token pricing invites a race to the bottom against
  whoever bundles inference cheapest.
- **Per-seat for auditors.** External verification must stay free; charging
  auditors to check receipts would invert the trust model.

## Computed economics (first-pass hypotheses)

| Metric | Hypothesis basis | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin | Control plane is software; COGS = support + infra | ≥ 70% |
| NRR | Land one workflow, expand to security/compliance/model governance | ≥ 110% |
| CAC payback | Founder-led outbound, no paid acquisition initially | ≤ 18 mo |
| Burn multiple | Solo + contractors until $3.5M ARR path is visible | ≤ 1.5x |

These become real the moment the first design partner signs. Until then they
are the numbers we test against, published so they can be attacked.
